# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique work, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Give and receive feedback on technical merit.
- Assume the person you disagree with has a reason; ask for it.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change and explain why.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, or publishing others' private
information. Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

Applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any space where
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers privately. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate.
